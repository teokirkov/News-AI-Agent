# Source Bias Ratings

A general-reference table of editorial lean for commonly cited outlets.
Used to add bias context to citations in the Report Page — **not** a
precise or scientific measurement, just a widely-recognized general
positioning to help readers understand the spread of coverage.

**Scale used:** Left · Lean Left · Center · Lean Right · Right · Wire/Neutral

> Wire services (Reuters, AP, AFP) are rated "Wire/Neutral" — their core
> news reporting is generally produced to strict neutrality standards,
> distinct from opinion/editorial content they may also publish.

## How to update this table
- If a story cites an outlet not listed here, don't guess a rating —
  mark it "Unrated" in the report itself, and optionally add a
  best-effort entry here afterward if a reliable third-party media-bias
  reference (e.g. AllSides, Ad Fontes Media) confirms a rating.
- Ratings can shift over time; revisit periodically rather than treating
  this as permanent.
- This table is a practical heuristic for transparency, not an
  authoritative judgment — always feel free to adjust entries.
