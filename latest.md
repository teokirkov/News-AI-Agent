## 1. Flash floods on Nepal-Tibet border kill 157+, hundreds of foreign tourists missing
*World / Disaster*

An ice-and-rock avalanche off a glacier on the Nepal-China border blocked the Bhotekoshi (Lende Khola) river on August 26, triggering a sudden surge that destroyed villages, roads and hydropower infrastructure in Nepal's Rasuwa and Nuwakot districts and China's Gyirong County. Confirmed deaths climbed sharply over 24 hours, and by the latest reports at least 157 people had died with hundreds more missing — including nearly 500 foreign nationals from 28 countries, among them trekkers and pilgrims from India, the US, Ukraine, Australia, Britain, Malaysia and Canada. China's state broadcaster separately reported at least 3 dead and 265 missing on its side of the border. Scientists say the disaster fits a pattern of accelerating glacial instability in the Himalayas linked to climate change.

> **Coverage note:** Death toll figures moved fast and diverged significantly as the story developed — early reports cited roughly 72–95 dead; later reporting on Aug 27 put the confirmed toll at 157+. Treat casualty figures as provisional and still rising.
