# Preferred Sources

Outlets trusted by default for this report. Add/remove as you refine your
preferences. Cross-reference `resources/source-bias-ratings.md` for each
outlet's lean when citing.

## Wire services (generally Wire/Neutral)
- Reuters
- Associated Press (AP)
- Agence France-Presse (AFP)

## International / national news
- BBC
- The New York Times
- The Wall Street Journal
- The Washington Post
- The Guardian
- Financial Times
- NPR
- Axios
- Politico

## Business & markets
- Bloomberg
- Financial Times
- CNBC

## Science & tech
- Nature (news section)
- Ars Technica
- The Verge (for tech industry news)

## Official/primary sources
- Government press releases, central bank statements, official
  institutional announcements (e.g. WHO, UN, Federal Reserve)

## Notes for multi-source stories
- When gathering 3–5 sources per story, try to draw from more than one
  section of this list where possible (e.g. not five outlets from the
  same lean bucket) — see `resources/source-bias-ratings.md`.
- Wire services are a strong "anchor" source for factual grounding but
  shouldn't be the only source when others are available, since they
  don't provide lean diversity on their own.
