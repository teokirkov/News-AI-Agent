### Nepal–Tibet glacial flood disaster
- First appeared: 2026-08-27 report (as #1)
- Status: ongoing
- Notes: Glacier fracture on Mount Langtang Lirung on Aug 26, 2026 triggered
  an ice-rock avalanche and debris flow. Death toll trajectory: 157+ (Aug 27)
  → 1,000+ (Sep 1). Missing counts vary widely between sources; treat as
  provisional every week.

### US–Iran confrontation / Strait of Hormuz
- First appeared: 2026-08-27 report (as #2, sanctions phase)
- Status: ongoing
- Notes: "Operation Economic Outcast" sanctions campaign from ~Aug 24, then a
  return to direct military exchange on Aug 30–31 after a roughly month-long
  pause. A June 2026 interim agreement is the reference point both sides cite.
  Watch: Brent crude, Hormuz transit volumes, tanker/mine incidents.

### NASA Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope
- First appeared: 2026-08-27 report (as #10, pre-launch)
- Status: ongoing (cruise phase)
- Notes: Launched Aug 30, 2026 on Falcon Heavy. Roughly three-month cruise to
  its operating orbit — expect a "science operations begin" milestone around
  late Nov / Dec 2026.

### Sudan civil war (RSF vs. army)
- First appeared: 2026-09-01 report (as #10)
- Status: ongoing
- Notes: Kordofan and Blue Nile are the active fronts. Chronically
  under-covered by Western national outlets; casualty figures usually
  originate from local medical groups and are rarely independently verified.

### DR Congo Ebola outbreak (Bundibugyo strain)
- First appeared: 2026-09-01 report (as #4)
- Status: ongoing
- Notes: Fastest-spreading Ebola epidemic on record per the UN. No approved
  vaccine for the Bundibugyo strain; Ervebo being used off-label under
  compassionate use. Uganda declared Ebola-free Aug 28, 2026.

### US–Venezuela oil arrangement
- First appeared: 2026-09-01 report (as #3)
- Status: ongoing
- Notes: Pentagon equity stake in North American Blue Energy Partners; terms
  reported inconsistently across outlets (20% at-cost purchase right vs. 55%
  of effective output). Worth re-checking as the text becomes public.
