# Excluded Sources

Outlets/types of sources to avoid citing in the digest.

## Categories to avoid
- Content-farm / SEO-only sites with no original reporting
- Aggregator sites that just republish other outlets without attribution
- Anonymous or unattributed blogs presented as news
- Satire sites (e.g. The Onion) presented as straight news
- Sites with a strong track record of misinformation or frequent
  unretracted factual errors
